Method. Implied value = TVL × fitted multiple × growth × maturity. The multiple is fitted from the snapshot itself: the median market-cap/TVL ratio of the tokenised protocols in the same category (shrunk toward the global median, k = 5), winsorised to [0.005, 2.0]. Growth is the 7-day TVL change clamped to [−60, +120]%, weighted 0.5; maturity rises with days listed, 0.6 to 1.0 over three years. The fit is tested blind on a 20% holdout of the comps (see the model panel). No protocol name is ever special-cased; every number derives from the snapshot's own fields.

Reading it. “Records no token” means the snapshot's DefiLlama record carries no token — never that the protocol has none. Implied value is this entry's own claim about what a launch would be worth if the protocol's record stayed as it is; it does not predict whether or when a token is issued. Farm steps are generic guidance, not financial advice; airdrops are never guaranteed.
